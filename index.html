A diversified, six-channel approach to durable funding.

Resilient nonprofits don't depend on a single source. SEED's two-year plan balances six funder ecosystems — so momentum in one channel never leaves the mission exposed in another.

The Partnership

Shop the store ↗

The Marathon Clothing — the smart-store apparel brand founded by Nipsey Hussle, his brother Blacc Sam, and longtime collaborator Steve Carless — reimagined the corner shop as community ownership infrastructure. Opened on Slauson Avenue in 2017, every drop is a milestone in a longer race.

In partnership with SEED, that same ethos — building locally, owning your block, and finishing what you start — becomes a bridge from Crenshaw to the continent. Co-branded drops and cultural moments help fuel SEED scholarships and the next generation of African leaders. The marathon continues.
